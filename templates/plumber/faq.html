Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you call.

How quickly can you attend an emergency?

We aim to reach you within 1–2 hours for active leaks or flooding, subject to traffic and workload.

Do you charge call‑out fees?

No call‑out fee when work proceeds. We only charge for parts and labour after inspection.

Are your quotes fixed?

Yes – we provide a firm price before starting any work. No surprises.

What areas of Dublin do you cover?

All Dublin postcode areas, including surrounding counties on urgent jobs.
