Leak Detection in Dublin

Signs of a Hidden Leak
  • Damp patches on walls/ceilings
  • Mould or musty smells
  • Staining near radiators/pipe runs
  • Unexplained high water bills
  • Low pressure or noisy pipework
Our approach

We narrow down likely causes, isolate where needed, locate the source, and complete repairs with a clean finish.

Leak Detection FAQ

Do you repair the leak as well?
In most cases, yes — once located, we’ll explain options and repair where possible.

Will you need to open walls?
Sometimes. We aim to minimise disruption and confirm the plan before work starts.

Is it urgent?
Leaks can cause ongoing damage. If you suspect an active leak, call as soon as possible.
