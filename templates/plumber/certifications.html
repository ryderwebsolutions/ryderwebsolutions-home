Our Certifications

Professional qualifications that give you confidence in our workmanship.

We are fully qualified and registered with the following Irish plumbing and gas‑fitting bodies:

  • Registered Plumbers Board of Ireland (CDBB registration)
  • Gas Safe Register – competent to work on domestic and commercial appliances
  • CIRI (Chartered Institute of Plumbing and Heating Engineers, Ireland) member
  • RECI‑approved installers for renewable heating systems
  • Health & Safety Authority (HSA) certified for confined spaces and manual handling
  • SafePQQ qualified for public sector contracts
  • WaterSafe Ireland listing – approved for cross‑connection control and backflow prevention
  • Fully insured with public liability cover (€6 million) and employer’s liability

Valid certificates and insurance documents available upon request or viewable at our office.
