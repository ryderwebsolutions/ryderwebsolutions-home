Traditional Thai massage is performed fully clothed — we provide comfortable, loose-fitting attire if needed. For oil-based treatments like aromatherapy or deep tissue, you'll be appropriately draped with towels throughout for your modesty and comfort.

Thai massage involves firm pressure and stretching, which can feel intense in tight areas — but it should never cause sharp pain. We always work within your comfort level and encourage you to communicate throughout. Most people feel a wonderful mix of intensity and relief, describing it as "good pain."
