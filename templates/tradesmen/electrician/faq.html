Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you call.

How quickly can you attend an emergency?

We aim to reach urgent electrical faults as quickly as possible, typically within 1-2 hours depending on traffic and live workload. We will confirm an ETA when you call.

What should I do if power is out in part of the house?

First check whether one breaker or the main switch has tripped. If safe, switch off sensitive appliances and reset the breaker once. If it trips again, stop and call an electrician.

Do you charge call-out fees?

No call-out fee when work proceeds. If a technician attends and no work is approved, an attendance charge may apply and will be explained upfront.

Can you fix tripping breakers and RCD faults?

Yes. We trace the fault, isolate the affected circuit, and repair the underlying issue so the problem does not keep returning.

Do I need a full rewire?

Not always. Some homes only need partial rewiring or a fuse board upgrade. We inspect first and recommend the safest, most cost-effective option.

Are your quotes fixed?

Yes. We provide clear pricing before work begins. Any additional work is discussed and approved by you first.

What areas of Dublin do you cover?

We cover Dublin 1-24 and surrounding areas. For urgent electrical issues, call us and we will confirm the fastest available attendance.

Do you install EV chargers and lighting?

Yes. We install EV charging points, indoor and outdoor lighting, and can upgrade existing circuits where needed for safe operation.

What if I smell burning from sockets or switches?

Turn off power to the affected circuit immediately if safe to do so, avoid using the outlet, and call us right away. Burning smells can indicate a serious fault.
