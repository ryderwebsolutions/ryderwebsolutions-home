Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you call.

How quickly can you attend an emergency?

We aim to reach you within 1–2 hours for active leaks or flooding, subject to traffic and workload. In most cases a technician will call you en route to give an estimated arrival time.

What should I do if I have a leak?

First, turn off the water supply at the stopcock (usually under the kitchen sink or where the mains enters the property). If you cannot locate it, turn off the mains at the street valve and contact us immediately.

Simple leak check:

  1. Close all taps in the house.
  2. Watch your water meter; if it keeps moving, there is a leak somewhere.
  3. Feel exposed pipes for dampness or listen near fittings for dripping sounds.
  4. If the leak is visible, place a bucket beneath to limit damage and call us.
Do you charge call‑out fees?

No call‑out fee when work proceeds. We only charge for parts and labour after inspection. If you call out a technician and decide not to proceed, a small attendance fee may apply – this will be explained up front.

How do I reset my boiler?

Most modern boilers have a reset button on the front panel.

1. Turn the power off for 30 seconds.
2. Press and hold the reset button until the display clears (usually 5–10 seconds).
3. Wait 5 minutes to allow the boiler to purge air and restart.

If the fault code returns or the boiler trips again, there may be a deeper issue – give us a call and provide the error code shown on the display.

What happens with frozen pipes?

Frozen pipes can burst if left. To thaw safely:

  1. Turn off the water supply.
  2. Open the affected tap to relieve pressure.
  3. Apply gentle heat using a hot water bottle, cloth soaked in hot water or a hairdryer set to low.
  4. Do not use a naked flame.

Once thawed inspect for leaks. If you notice water escaping, call us straight away.

Are your quotes fixed?

Yes – we provide a firm price before starting any work. No surprises. Any additional work required will only proceed after your approval.

What areas of Dublin do you cover?

All Dublin postcode areas, including surrounding counties on urgent jobs. We can frequently reach north county Dublin and parts of Wicklow for emergencies.

How can I check my radiators are working?

Feel each radiator; it should be warm from top to bottom. If the bottom stays cool:

  1. Turn off the heating and allow system to cool.
  2. Use a radiator key to bleed air from the valve until water appears.
  3. Top up the boiler pressure if it has dropped (check the gauge on the boiler).

Still cold? There may be sludge; call us for a power‑flush or servicing.

How do I report emergency water quality issues?

If your water is discoloured, has sediment or a bad smell:

  1. Run a cold tap for 5 minutes.
  2. Check with your neighbours to see if it’s local.
  3. Contact Irish Water on 1800 278 278 if the problem persists.
