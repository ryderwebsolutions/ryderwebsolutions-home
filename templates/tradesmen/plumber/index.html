FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
How do heat pumps work?
Heat pumps extract heat from outside air or ground even in cold weather, then amplify it indoors for heating. The Viessmann Vitocal 151-A is highly efficient and works well with underfloor heating systems.
What are the benefits of a heat pump system?
Heat pumps reduce heating costs by 50-70%, lower carbon emissions, qualify for SEAI grants up to 12,500, and integrate seamlessly with renewable energy. They provide consistent, quiet heating year-round.
Can I get an SEAI grant for a heat pump?
Yes. Subject to eligibility, you can receive up to 12,500 from the SEAI Better Energy Homes scheme. We handle all grant paperwork and ensure your installation meets SEAI requirements for maximum support.
How long does a heat pump installation take?
Most Viessmann Vitocal installations take 3-5 days depending on your property size and existing systems. We'll provide a detailed timeline during your consultation.
Is underfloor heating compatible with heat pumps?
Yes. Underfloor heating is ideal with heat pumps as it operates at lower water temperatures, maximizing efficiency and comfort. We design systems to optimize both components together.
Are heat pumps noisy?
The Viessmann Vitocal 151-A is engineered for quiet operation, typically 45-50 decibels. Most customers report it's less noisy than traditional boilers and barely noticeable during normal use.
What maintenance do heat pumps require?
Heat pump systems require minimal maintenance. Annual filter checks and periodic inspections ensure optimal performance. We offer maintenance packages to keep your system running efficiently.
Can you design a heating system for my property?
Absolutely. Our SEAI and RGI registered engineers assess your property's heating needs, design custom systems with heat pumps and underfloor heating, and handle all installation and grant support.
