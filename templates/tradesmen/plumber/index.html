FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
🚨 What should I do in a plumbing emergency?
Call us immediately at 087 254 5939. We're available 24/7 for emergencies. We'll dispatch a plumber as quickly as possible and provide emergency guidance over the phone while en route.
⚡ How quickly can you respond to emergencies?
We aim to respond to emergency calls within 30–60 minutes depending on location. For urgent issues like burst pipes or major leaks, we prioritize rapid dispatch to minimize water damage.
💷 How much does an emergency call-out cost?
Emergency call-out fees start at a base rate, with additional charges for labor and materials used. We provide a quote before starting work. Contact us on 087 254 5939 for current rates.
📅 How often should I have my plumbing inspected?
We recommend professional plumbing inspections every 2–3 years, especially for older properties. Regular maintenance prevents costly emergencies and identifies issues early.
🚿 What causes blocked drains?
Common causes include hair, soap buildup, tree roots, food debris, and mineral deposits. Our drain specialists use advanced diagnostics and clearing methods to solve blockages quickly.
🔧 Do you provide drain cleaning services?
Yes. We offer professional drain cleaning, CCTV inspections, and root removal. We can clear blockages and identify underlying pipe issues before they become major problems.
🔥 How long do boiler repairs typically take?
Simple repairs may take 1–2 hours, while more complex issues can take longer. During your consultation, we'll provide an estimated timeline. Emergency boiler issues are our priority.
✓ Do you offer warranty on plumbing work?
Yes. All our plumbing work comes with professional warranty. We stand behind our installations and repairs, ensuring quality and peace of mind for our customers.
