FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is a Viessmann Vitocal 151-A?
The Vitocal 151-A is a high-efficiency air-source heat pump from Viessmann. It extracts heat from outdoor air to provide heating and hot water. It's highly efficient, eligible for SEAI grants, and ideal for Irish homes.
How much can I save with a heat pump?
Savings depend on your current heating method and home insulation. Heat pumps typically reduce heating costs by 40–70% compared to oil or gas boilers. We'll assess your home and provide accurate projections.
Am I eligible for a SEAI grant?
Most homeowners in qualifying areas are eligible for up to €12,500 in SEAI grant support. Eligibility depends on home age, location, and current heating system. We'll assess this during your free consultation.
How long does installation take?
A typical heat pump installation takes 3–5 days depending on system complexity and your home setup. Underfloor heating retrofits may take longer. We'll provide a clear timeline during the quote stage.
Do I need underfloor heating with a heat pump?
No. Heat pumps work with existing radiators or underfloor systems. However, underfloor heating maximizes heat pump efficiency. We'll recommend the best option for your home during the design phase.
What about noise from heat pumps?
Modern Viessmann heat pumps run quietly — typically 40–50 dB, similar to an air conditioning unit. Proper placement and professional installation ensure minimal noise impact.
Is system balancing necessary?
Yes. System balancing optimizes water flow to each radiator or zone, ensuring even heating and maximum efficiency. It's a professional service we provide for all new and upgraded systems.
Do you offer warranty on installations?
Yes. All our installations include professional warranty and follow manufacturer guidelines. We're SEAI and RGI certified, ensuring compliance with standards.
